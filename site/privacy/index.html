The short version

Your videos, photos, recordings, presentations and transcripts are made and kept on your own devices, and in the folder you choose, such as iCloud or Google Drive. We never receive them. We only keep what is needed for your account and your subscription.

Who we are

Transformation Studios is published by Ryan Caley, Toulouse, France [TO CONFIRM] (legal name or company, and postal address). For anything about your data, write to hello@transformationstudio.site.

What runs on your device only

Speech-to-text and captions run on your device. Editing and exporting also happen on your device. [TO CONFIRM] (that translation and voice clean-up also run on the device, on iPhone and iPad as well as Mac)

Services we use

  • Apple. The App Store is the seller: it handles payment, VAT and invoices. Apple receives your payment details directly and we never see your card. We store the subscription status Apple reports so the app can unlock. Apple is also used if you sign in with Apple.
  • Supabase. Holds accounts and subscription status. Servers in [TO CONFIRM] (region).
  • Google. Only if you choose to sign in with Google, or to import from or save to Google Drive.

Features where you send data yourself

  • Google Drive and iCloud folders. If you keep your library there, or import a script from there, Google or Apple stores those files under your agreement with them.
  • Ask Claude (presentations, on Mac). The text you type is sent to Anthropic using your own Anthropic API key, which is stored in your Mac's Keychain. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to that request.
  • iPhone and iPad link. Works on your own Wi-Fi only, directly between your devices.
  • Sharing. When you post or share a file, it goes to the app or service you pick.

This website

This website sets no cookies and has no visitor tracking. Our host, Netlify, keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and the page requested) to run and protect the site. If you play the story video, it loads from the video service only after you click. If you email us, we keep your message so we can reply.

How long we keep it

Account data: while your account exists, then deleted within 30 days of you deleting it. Subscription status: while your account exists. Apple keeps the payment invoices.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, object to processing, and complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr). You can ask for your account to be deleted from inside the app [TO CONFIRM] (where the Delete My Account option is on iPhone and iPad; on Mac it is Help › Delete My Account…) or by writing to hello@transformationstudio.site. We do it within 30 days.

Children

Transformation Studios is not intended for children under 15 without a parent's consent.

Changes

We will tell you in the app or by email before any important change.
