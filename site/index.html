Questions

Does one subscription cover iPhone, iPad and Mac?

Yes. Subscribe once with your Apple ID and use them all.

No. It was built for one person and a phone. Smart Cut and auto captions do the slow parts.

Anything your phone shares to: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, WhatsApp and more, in 9:16, 16:9, 1:1 or 4:5.
