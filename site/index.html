Questions

Does one subscription cover iPhone, iPad and Mac?

Yes. Subscribe once with your Apple Account and use them all.

Do I need editing experience?

No. It was built for one person and a phone. Smart Cut and auto captions do the slow parts.

Which platforms can I post to?

Anything your phone shares to: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, WhatsApp and more, in 9:16, 16:9, 1:1 or 4:5.

Is my content private?

Yes. Speech-to-text and captions run on your device, and your videos and recordings stay on it.

Which languages does the app speak?

English, French, Spanish and German.

Can I subscribe even if I won't use the app?

Yes. It's the same subscription, and it resources the mission. The yearly plan suits this best: a full year of support, and the app is yours whether you use it or not.

What is the Founding 100?

The first 100 people to join the mission. You can join as a Creator (monthly), a Partner (yearly) or an Ambassador (you share the link, at no cost). Every partner stays anonymous.

Is my subscription a donation?

No. It's an app purchase, and it is not tax-deductible. The income helps support Ryan and Marita's missionary work in France.
