Cancel your subscription

Your subscription is managed by Apple, so you cancel it in your Apple ID settings. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

  • iPhone or iPad. Open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Choose Transformation Studios and tap Cancel Subscription.
  • Mac. Open the App Store, click your name, then Account Settings. Under Subscriptions click Manage, then Edit next to Transformation Studios and Cancel Subscription.
  • Any browser. Go to apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Restore your subscription

If you changed device or reinstalled the app, sign in with the same Apple ID you subscribed with, open the subscription screen in the app and choose Restore Purchases. [TO CONFIRM] (the exact name and place of the Restore button)
