Contact for anything about your data: **admin@transformationstudio.site**.

## The short version
Your videos, photos, recordings, scripts, transcripts and translations are made and kept **on your device**
(and in the folder you choose, such as Google Drive or iCloud Drive). We never receive them unless you share them yourself.
We only keep what is needed for your account and your subscription. We do not track you and we show no ads.

## What we collect
| What | Why | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|
| Email address, and name if you sign in with Apple or Google | To create and sign in to your account | Contract |
| Account identifier and sign-in times | To keep you signed in securely | Contract / legitimate interest |
| Subscription status and renewal date (reported by Apple) | To unlock the app on your iPhone, iPad and Mac | Contract |
| Messages you send to us | To answer you | Legitimate interest |

We **do not** collect your videos, photos or recordings, your projects, what you say in your recordings, analytics of what you do in the
app, or advertising identifiers. We do not use crash-reporting or analytics services. We do not track you across other companies' apps and websites.
We do not sell or share personal data for advertising.

## Do I need an account?
Yes. On iPhone, iPad and Mac you sign in (with Apple, Google or email) so that one subscription unlocks all your devices.

## Permissions on iPhone and iPad
- **Camera**: to film your videos in the app.
- **Microphone**: to record your voice with your videos and for voice notes. A voice-note recording keeps going if the screen locks.
- **Speech recognition**: for captions, transcripts, cutting pauses and a teleprompter that follows your voice (see below).
- **Photos**: to save your finished videos to your Photos library, and to add photos you choose yourself. You decide how much access the app has.

Recordings, photos and videos stay on your device unless you choose to share or export them.

## Speech to text and translation
On iPhone and iPad, speech to text uses Apple's speech recognition. When your device supports it, the audio is
processed **on the device**. When it does not (some languages or older devices), Apple's speech recognition may send
the audio to **Apple's servers** under Apple's privacy terms; we never receive it. The first time you use a language, iOS may download
Apple's speech model for it. On the Mac, speech to text runs on the Mac (models downloaded once). Translation uses Apple Translation on your device.

## Services we use (processors)
- **Supabase**: accounts and subscription status. Servers in **Ireland (EU)**.
- **Apple**: the App Store is the seller (payments, VAT and invoices). Apple receives payment details directly; we never see your card. We store the subscription status Apple reports so one subscription unlocks iPhone, iPad and Mac. Apple is also used if you sign in with Apple.
- **Google**: only if you choose to sign in with Google.
- **Netlify**: hosts our website. Like any web host, it sees your IP address and browser details when you visit. The Mac app also asks the website for a small update list now and then; no account or personal details are sent.

## Features where you send data yourself
- **Google Drive / iCloud Drive folders**: if you save your library there, Google or Apple stores those files under your agreement with them.
- **Sharing**: when you share a video to another app, that app's privacy policy applies.

## Deleting your account
In the iPhone and iPad app: **Home › Account (top of the screen) › Delete account › Delete account and data**.
Cancel an App Store subscription first in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions: deleting the account does not cancel it.
Deleting removes your account and plan records, and revokes the Sign in with Apple link. The settings kept on the device are cleared; the videos you made stay on your device.
On the Mac: Help › Delete My Account… You can also write to admin@transformationstudio.site.

## How long we keep it
Account data: while your account exists, then deleted within 30 days of you deleting it.
Subscription status: while your account exists. Apple keeps the payment invoices.
Messages to us: up to 3 years after our last exchange.

## Your rights
You can access, correct, export or delete your data, object to processing, and complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr), the French data protection authority.
Write to admin@transformationstudio.site; we answer within 30 days.

## Children
Transformation Studio is not intended for children under 15 without a parent's consent.

## Changes
We'll tell you in the app or by email before any important change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
